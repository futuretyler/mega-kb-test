parser: the shared parser reads every date month first

A day-first tenant writes 7 April as 07/04. The parser reads it as 4 July, and the invite and the reminder go out on that day. It happens on every channel and for every tenant on file. This is the fix that is queued for #4471.

hint: the booking-v3 form tells day-first tenants to type the month first

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. The tenant’s people type what the hint says, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. The date flips before the parser sees it. It happens only through the form of the tenants that run booking-v3. The parser fix does not change it: the hint and the reading of the form must agree.

one-day: dates move by one day, not by a flip

These reports do not show a month and a day swapped. A Tuesday shows as a Monday, every booking in June is one day off, and one meeting came out on two different days for two clients in two cities. One invite arrived before the booking was made. That points to time zones or summer time, not to the order of day and month. The desk noted these reports as a one-day move (desk-notes.json) but counted them under parser.

How to read a report

  • Read the body. The subject line and the intake tag are not the cause. Intake guesses the tag from the reporter’s browser, and the browser is often not the tenant’s locale.
  • The export repeats some reports on overlapping pages. Count each report id once.
  • When the cause is not clear, keep the report on its own ticket and say why.
